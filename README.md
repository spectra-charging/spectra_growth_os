# Spectra Growth OS

**AI-powered Marketing Intelligence & Automation Platform**

Developed under Spectra Energy Networks Private Limited.

## Overview

Spectra Growth OS is an AI-powered platform designed to help energy and renewable energy businesses improve their marketing operations.

The platform aims to simplify market research, content creation, campaign planning and marketing performance analysis.

## Target Industry

- Solar energy
- Renewable energy
- Energy infrastructure
- Energy services
- Power and charging solutions

## Planned Modules

| Module | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Company Intelligence | Understand company profile and brand identity |
| Market Intelligence | Research competitors and market trends |
| AI Content Studio | Generate marketing content |
| Creative Assistant | Support branded creative generation |
| Campaign Management | Plan and manage campaigns |
| Marketing Analytics | Track marketing performance |
